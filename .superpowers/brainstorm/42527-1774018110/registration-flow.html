Registration & Payment Flow

What a parent sees when joining the club

1
Parent visits Register page
Clicks "Register your child" — fills in player name, age, position, parent contact info
2
Account created
Supabase creates a parent account. They get an email to verify & set password.
3
Payment page (Stripe)
Shown the membership fee. Pays by credit card or PayPal. Stripe handles everything securely — card details never touch your server.
💳 Credit / Debit Card
🅿️ PayPal
4
Payment confirmed
Stripe notifies your site (webhook). Player is marked as active member. Parent gets a receipt by email automatically.
5
Access to member area
Parent & player can now log in to see their profile, payment history, and (later) announcements, schedule, and messaging.
🛡️ Meanwhile — you see it all in the Admin Dashboard
List of all registered players · Payment status (paid / unpaid) · Total revenue · One-click to mark someone as paid manually (if they pay cash)
